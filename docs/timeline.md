# Application Timeline

The EU AI Act entered into force on **1 August 2024** and applies in full from **2 August 2026**, with phased application of specific chapters. This phased approach gives stakeholders time to prepare.

| Date | Event | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| **12 July 2024** | Publication in the Official Journal of the EU (OJ L, 2024/1689) | Art 113 |
| **1 August 2024** | Entry into force (20th day after publication) | Art 113 |
| **2 February 2025** | **Chapters I and II apply:** general provisions, definitions, and prohibited AI practices (Article 5) | Art 113(a) |
| **2 August 2025** | **Chapter III Section 4** (notified bodies), **Chapter V** (GPAI models), **Chapter VII** (governance), **Chapter XII** (final provisions) and **Article 78** (confidentiality) apply, except Article 101 | Art 113(b) |
| **2 August 2026** | **Full application** of the Regulation (all remaining provisions) | Art 113 |
| **2 August 2027** | **Article 6(1)** and corresponding obligations apply (high-risk classification for AI systems that are safety components of products under Annex I) | Art 113(c) |

---

## What each phase means

### Phase 1 — 2 February 2025 (Chapters I & II)
- General provisions (subject matter, scope, definitions).
- **Prohibited AI practices** (Article 5) become enforceable.
- AI literacy obligations (Article 4).

### Phase 2 — 2 August 2025
- **Notified bodies** (Chapter III Section 4): designation and requirements.
- **GPAI models** (Chapter V): obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models.
- **Governance** (Chapter VII): AI Office, European AI Board, Advisory Forum, Scientific Panel.
- **Confidentiality** (Article 78).

### Phase 3 — 2 August 2026 (full application)
- All remaining provisions, including:
  - High-risk system obligations (Articles 9–27).
  - Conformity assessment, CE marking, registration.
  - Transparency obligations (Article 50).
  - Market surveillance and enforcement.
  - Penalties (Article 99).

### Phase 4 — 2 August 2027
- **Article 6(1)** high-risk classification for AI systems that are safety components of products covered by Annex I harmonisation legislation.
