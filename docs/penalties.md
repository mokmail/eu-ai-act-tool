# Penalties

The EU AI Act establishes a tiered system of administrative fines. The exact amounts are set by Member States (for national enforcement) and by the Commission (for GPAI models and Union institutions), within the caps set by the Regulation.

| Ref | Violation | Fine |
|---|---|---|
| **Art 99(3)** | Non-compliance with **prohibited practices** (Article 5) | Up to **EUR 35,000,000** or **7%** of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher |
| **Art 99(4)** | Non-compliance with obligations of **providers** (Art 16), **authorised representatives** (Art 22), **importers** (Art 23), **distributors** (Art 24), **deployers** (Art 26), **notified bodies** (Arts 31, 33, 34), or **transparency obligations** (Art 50) | Up to **EUR 15,000,000** or **3%** of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher |
| **Art 99(5)** | Supply of **incorrect, incomplete or misleading information** to notified bodies or national competent authorities | Up to **EUR 7,500,000** or **1%** of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher |
| **Art 99(6)** | **SMEs / start-ups** | Member States may cap fines at the lower of the above amounts |
| **Art 100** | Infringements by **Union institutions, bodies, offices and agencies** | Fines set by the Commission, up to the amounts in Article 99 |
| **Art 101** | Non-compliance by **GPAI model providers** (Art 53) or **systemic-risk providers** (Art 55), or failure to comply with Commission requests (Arts 91, 92, 93) | Up to **EUR 15,000,000** or **3%** of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher (imposed by the Commission) |

---

## Key points

- **Turnover basis:** Fines are calculated on the *total worldwide annual turnover* of the preceding financial year, not just EU turnover.
- **SME protection:** Article 99(6) allows Member States to cap fines for SMEs and start-ups at the lower of the applicable amounts, reflecting the Act's aim to avoid disproportionate burdens on smaller operators.
- **GPAI enforcement:** For general-purpose AI models, the **Commission** (not Member States) imposes fines under Article 101, reflecting the EU-level governance of GPAI models.
- **Effectiveness, proportionality, dissuasiveness:** Article 99(1) requires that penalties be effective, proportionate and dissuasive, taking into account the interests of SMEs and start-ups.
